# Feature: Display Custom Song Metadata in Game Song List

(see [goal.md](goal.md) for the high-level goals and background) 

Right now, we can target an existing song in Beat Saber on the PS4 and replace it with a custom song of our choice, but the custom song just appears in the game as the original song we replaced, with the original song's title, artist, album, album art, and special modes (like 90-degree or one-saber or no-arrows modes) displayed in the song selection menu.

The goal of this feature is to replace the original song in the game with a custom song of our choice, and have the game display the custom song's title, artist, album, and album art instead of the original song's title, artist, album, album art, and special modes (like 90-degree or one-saber or no-arrows modes) displayed in the song selection menu.
